About Prem Foundation

Committed to dignity, care, and community service

Our Mission

To provide compassionate care and sustainable solutions for underserved communities through holistic programs that address physical, emotional, and social needs.

Our Vision

A society where every individual ages with dignity, and no one is left behind due to socioeconomic barriers. We envision empowered communities supporting participatory approaches.

Our Journey

P R E M Foundation
Participatory Research and Environment Management Organisation

Since 2012, PREM Foundation has pioneered sustainable development through participatory approaches. Our journey reflects a commitment to environmental stewardship and community empowerment, creating lasting impact through research-driven solutions.

Our Impact & Achievements

~ Another World Is Possible

Women Livelihood Empowerment

Comprehensive program supporting 1,800 tribal women across Nashik and Nandurbar districts with:

  • Business upgradation support
  • Organic farming promotion (100+ acres)
  • Skill training and capacity building
  • Establishment of "NARI" women's private limited company

Environmental Conservation

Sustainable initiatives focused on preserving natural resources:

  • Watershed development projects
  • 10,000+ trees planted for environmental conservation
  • Promotion of climate-resilient agricultural practices
  • Natural resource management training
